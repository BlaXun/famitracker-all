Help menu

Tutorials
Short tours of FamiTracker. A tutorial points at one part of the window at a time, says what it's for, and goes on when you've done what it says.

Example songs
Five short songs that explain themselves: a first song on the NES's own chip, the NES's classic tricks, the VRC6 chip, soft sounds in stereo, and ten sound effects for a game (a jump, a coin, a laser and more), made the NES's way, with how to make your own and export them for a game. Each opens as a new song with a walkthrough that points at the channel to listen to, plays a few bars at a time and lets one part be heard alone. Change anything you like: saving asks for a name, so the example stays as it is.
