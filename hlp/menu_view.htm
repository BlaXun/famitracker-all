View menu

Toolbar
Displays or hides the toolbar.

Status Bar
Displays or hides the status bar.

Control panel
Displays or hides the control panel.

Pattern editor
Displays or hides the pattern editor, the channel grid. While it's hidden, the piano roll fills its place: the note keys play notes in it, and with editing on (Space) they type them at the cursor line. One of the pattern editor and the piano roll always shows.

Piano roll
Displays or hides the piano roll under the pattern editor: the notes on a grid, time from left to right. Hiding it while the pattern editor is hidden brings the pattern editor back.

Drum grid
Displays or hides the drum grid (also in the Edit menu, Ctrl+Shift+D): the noise channel's drums, a bar at a time, for the bar the cursor is in. Right-click it to dock it on the right of the window, or at the bottom again. The views shown, the piano roll's height and the drum grid's place come back when FamiTracker starts.

Frame editor position
Moves the frame editor to the top (default) or to the left of the pattern editor.
