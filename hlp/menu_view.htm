View menu

Toolbar
Displays or hides the toolbar.

Status Bar
Displays or hides the status bar.

Control panel
Displays or hides the control panel.

Pattern editor
Displays or hides the pattern editor, the channel grid. While it's hidden, the piano roll fills its place: the note keys play notes in it, and with editing on (Space) they type them at the cursor line. One of the pattern editor and the piano roll always shows.

Piano roll
Displays or hides the piano roll under the pattern editor: the notes on a grid, time from left to right. Hiding it while the pattern editor is hidden brings the pattern editor back. The channel you edit is bright and the others are faint: a click puts a note in even over a faint note, and a double-click on a faint note edits its channel. Its Chords button (or H, or a right-click on the chord names under the bar numbers) puts a chord on the channels that play nothing there, a held note on each, for the bar at the cursor or the bars selected: one of the key's chords, the ones the parts play there, or one typed, like Am7, or a chord a bar, like C G Am F. Ctrl+Z takes it back.

Drum grid
Displays or hides the drum grid (also in the Edit menu, Ctrl+Shift+D): the noise channel's drums, a bar at a time, for the bar the cursor is in. Right-click it to dock it on the right of the window, or at the bottom again. Along the bottom it shares a row with the problems panel and What next, and the panels there get shorter on a short window, so the notes keep about a third of it. The views shown, the piano roll's height and the drum grid's place come back when FamiTracker starts.

Frame editor position
Moves the frame editor to the top (default) or to the left of the pattern editor.
