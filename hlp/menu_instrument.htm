Instrument menu

Sound browser... (Ctrl+Shift+L)
Every sound the song can use, to find, hear and add, grouped by kind: leads, basses, pads and chords, plucks, bells, keys, drums and effects, each kind with its count on the left. In each kind the ready-made sounds come first, then the sounds of your reference library game by game, then the song's own instruments; click a heading to fold it or open it again (a kind with many games starts with them folded). Type words in Find (bell, warm, bass, the name of a game) and pick where the sounds come from and the channel they play on: the counts and the groups follow. Click a sound, or its play mark, to hear it, and click it again to hear it again; the arrow keys play each sound they reach and the note keys play the one picked at their notes. A sound not in the song yet plays without being added. Add to the song keeps the one you like as an instrument. The window can be made bigger or smaller.

New instrument
Add a new instrument. The instrument type depends on the selected channel in the pattern editor.

Remove instrument
Remove selected instrument.

Clone instrument
Clone selected instrument.

Deep Clone Instrument
Clone selected instrument and associated sequences.

Load from file...
Load an instrument from a file (.fti).

Save to file...
Save selected instrument to a file (.fti).

Edit
Open the instrument editor for the selected instrument.

Listen
Hear the selected instrument without opening it: a short tune for a lead, a bass line for a bass, held notes for a pad, a beat for drums, on the channel the song plays it on most. It's also the speaker button under the instrument list.
