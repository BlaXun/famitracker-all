; Help > Exercises: a phrase that breathes
title: A phrase that breathes
about: Split a tune that never stops into two phrases, each ending with a rest or a held note.

step
show: piano-roll
play: bars 1-4
text: This tune runs on and on: a note on every step and never a pause. Listen: it's like someone talking without taking a breath.
wait: heard

step
show: piano-roll
play: bars 1-4
check: phrases
text: Give it two [[phrases|phrase]] of two bars each, bars 1-2 and bars 3-4, and end each one with a breath: a note held for two beats or more, or a [[rest]] of a beat.

  In the piano roll, drag the end of a note to make it longer (the notes after it make way), or right-click notes to take them away. Then press Check.
hint: The phrases end at the ends of bars 2 and 4. In bar 2, let its fifth note (B) last to the end of the bar; in bar 4, hold its fifth note (C) the same way.
answer: Pulse 1, bar 2: D3 E3 F3 D3 B2 - - -
answer: Pulse 1, bar 4: F3 G3 A3 F3 C4 - - -
