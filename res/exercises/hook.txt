title: A hook that comes back
about: Play a two-bar hook again, with a different ending.

step
show: piano-roll
play: bars 1-2
text: Bars 1-2 have a [[hook]]: a short tune that's easy to remember. Bars 3-4 have only the chords. Listen to the hook.
wait: heard

step
show: piano-roll
play: bars 1-4
check: hook
text: Play the hook again in bars 3-4, but end it another way. Hearing it again makes it stick; a new ending makes it go somewhere.

  In the piano roll, hold Ctrl and drag a box around the hook's notes to select them, copy them (Ctrl+C), then point the mouse at the start of bar 3 and paste (Ctrl+V). Then press Esc (a drag moves one note again, not all you pasted), change the last note or two of bar 4, and press Check.
hint: The hook ends on D, which sounds like a question. End the second time on C, the [[home note]], and it sounds like the answer.
answer: Pulse 1, bar 3: E4 - G4 E4 C4 - D4 E4 | D4 - B3 - C4 - - -
