title: Four bars over chords
about: Write a tune of four bars over chords that are ready, mostly by step, and end it at home.

step
show: piano-roll
play: bars 1-4
text: The [[chords|chord]] and the bass are ready: C, Am, F and G, then C again. Listen to them first. Your tune goes on top, on Pulse 1.
wait: heard

step
show: piano-roll
play: bars 1-4
check: four-bars
text: Write a tune of four bars on Pulse 1: click notes into the piano roll over the chords. A click on one of their faint notes puts a tune note there too; a double-click would edit the chords instead.

  Move mostly by [[step|step-leap]], to the next note up or down (a leap now and then is fine), and end on the [[home note]], C, in bar 4. Press Check when you like it.
hint: Start on E-4, above the chords, and walk up and down: E, D, E, F, G. Let each bar lean on its chord, and in bar 4 land on C and hold it. The lighter lanes are the key's notes.
answer: Pulse 1, bar 1, Lead: E4 - D4 - E4 F4 G4 - | A4 - G4 - E4 - - - | F4 - A4 - G4 - B4 - | C5 - - - - - - -
