; Help > Example songs: the walkthrough of soft-and-wide.ftm, which opens with it.
; The format is in Source/TutorialScript.h.

title: Soft and wide
about: This FamiTracker's own: the VRC6 Plus's soft layer, notes placed left and right, and the mixer's Soft and Space knobs. Headphones help. About six minutes.

step
show: window
text: This calm example shows three things only this FamiTracker has: a soft layer of extra channels, stereo, and a mixer that rounds sounds and gives them a room to ring in. Headphones help to hear them. It's opened as a new song: saving asks for a name, and the example stays as it is.

  The box in front holds the song's comments. Close it, then press Next.

step
show: pattern
play: bars 1-16
text: The whole song first, about three quarters of a minute: four bars of strings and a music box, the tune from bar 5, and a soft ending. Press Play.
wait: heard

step
show: channel Soft 1
play: bars 1-4
solo: Soft 1, Soft 2, Soft 3
text: The song uses the VRC6 Plus: the VRC6 with a soft layer under it, four more channels called Soft 1 to Soft 4, whose sounds are smooth waves. Here Soft 1, Soft 2 and Soft 3 hold the chords with a string pad: one note each, three together make a chord.

step
show: channel Pulse 1
play: bars 1-2
solo: Pulse 1
text: In the intro a music box plays on the 2A03's Pulse 1. With headphones, hear its notes go left, right, left: the effect 8 places a note between the speakers, 800 far left, 880 the middle, 8FF far right. These notes alternate between 833 and 8CC.

step
show: channel VRC6 Pulse 1
play: bars 5-8
solo: VRC6 Pulse 1
text: The tune comes in at bar 5 on the VRC6's first pulse, with an instrument that breathes in softly, then settles. It stays in the middle, between the speakers.

step
show: channel VRC6 Pulse 2
play: bars 5-8
solo: VRC6 Pulse 1, VRC6 Pulse 2
text: The VRC6's second pulse echoes the tune a beat later, softer and over on the right (8C0), so the echo doesn't blur the tune in the middle.

step
show: mixer
text: View > Mixer gives every channel a strip: a fader for its level, M and S to mute it or hear it alone, and two knobs, Soft and Space. Soft rounds a channel's sound off; Space sends it into a room, a hall in this song (the bar at the top chooses it), where it rings on after the notes.

  The tune's strip (Pulse 1, with VRC6 Plus under it) has Space 35, the music box's (Pulse 1, 2A03) 50 and the strings' 20; the bass has none.

step
show: mixer
play: bars 5-8
solo: VRC6 Pulse 1
text: Hear what Space does, on the tune alone: press Play and listen to each note ring on after it ends. Then double-click the Space knob on the tune's strip to turn it off and press Play again: now each note stops dry. Ctrl+Z puts the knob back. (Dragging a knob up or down turns it.)

step
show: channel Triangle
play: bars 9-12
text: All together in bars 9 to 12: the strings, the tune, its echo on the right, and the triangle's bass, one long note in every bar.

step
show: window
text: These things are this FamiTracker's own. Other FamiTrackers play the song in mono and without Soft and Space, and see the soft layer as a Namco N163 chip. File > Export for games keeps them all in the OGG files for your game.

  That's the last example. Help > Tutorials has the tours of FamiTracker itself.
