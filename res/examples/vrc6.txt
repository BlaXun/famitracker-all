; Help > Example songs: the walkthrough of vrc6.ftm, which opens with it.
; The format is in Source/TutorialScript.h.

title: The VRC6
about: The VRC6 chip's two extra pulses and its sawtooth, first as the bass and then as the lead. About five minutes.

step
show: window
text: This example is about the VRC6, a sound chip that some Famicom cartridges carried (Castlevania III's has one). It's opened as a new song, so change what you like: saving asks for a name, and the example stays as it is.

  The box in front holds the song's comments. Close it, then press Next.

step
show: pattern
play: bars 1-16
text: The VRC6 adds three channels, the last three columns: two more pulses and a sawtooth. The 2A03's own pulses rest in this song, so what you hear is mostly the VRC6. Press Play to hear it all once.
wait: heard

step
show: channel VRC6 Pulse 1
play: bars 3-4
solo: VRC6 Pulse 1, VRC6 Pulse 2
text: The VRC6's two pulses (its Pulse 1 and Pulse 2) play a duet in bars 1 to 8: the tune and a second voice under it.

  Their wave comes in eight widths instead of the 2A03's four. The tune's instrument 00 is a little short of round (37.5%); the second voice's instrument 01 is narrower and brighter (18.75%), a width the 2A03's pulses can't play.

step
show: channel Sawtooth
play: bars 1-2
solo: Sawtooth
text: The sawtooth has a full, buzzing sound none of the 2A03's channels has. In bars 1 to 8 it plays the bass with instrument 02: low notes, eight in every bar.

step
show: channel Sawtooth
play: bars 9-10
solo: Sawtooth
text: In bars 9 to 16 the same channel plays the tune instead, with instrument 03: higher notes, and a vibrato that comes in by itself on the long ones (it's part of the instrument). A sawtooth makes a strong lead as well as a bass.

step
show: channel Triangle
play: bars 9-12
solo: Triangle
text: With the sawtooth on the tune, the 2A03's triangle takes over the bass: the same bass line, rounder and softer than the sawtooth's.

step
show: channel VRC6 Pulse 2
play: bars 9-12
solo: VRC6 Pulse 1, VRC6 Pulse 2
text: And the two VRC6 pulses hold the chords under the tune now: one long note each in every bar, with instrument 04, which swells in gently.

step
show: pattern
play: bars 7-10
text: Hear the change in the middle of the song: in bar 9 the bass and the lead trade places.

step
show: menu Module
text: A song's chips are switched on in Module > Module properties: tick VRC6 and its three channels appear in the pattern editor. (This song has it on already.)

step
show: window
text: That's the VRC6. Try the sawtooth yourself: put the cursor in its column, pick instrument 02 or 03 and play the keys. Help > Example songs has two more: soft sounds in stereo, and sound effects for a game.
