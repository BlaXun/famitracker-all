; Help > Tutorials. The format is in Source/TutorialScript.h.

title: Sounds and drums
about: Pick instruments from the library, and put drums in with the drum grid. About five minutes.

step
show: instruments
text: Every note plays with an instrument: a sound with its own character, how it starts, fades out and wobbles. This list holds the song's instruments.

step
show: menu Instrument
text: Open the sound browser: Instrument > Sound browser (or Ctrl+Shift+L). It lists every sound the song can use: ready-made sounds for each kind of channel, your reference library's and the song's own.
wait: command instrument-library

step
show: window
text: In the browser, pick a kind of sound on the left (leads, basses, drums...) or type what you look for in Find (like bell, bass or snare), click a sound to hear it, and press Add to keep the one you like. Add a sound, then close the browser.
wait: instrument

step
show: instruments
text: Your new instrument is in the list. Click an instrument to choose it: the notes you type from now on play with it, and double-clicking one opens it for changing its sound. The speaker button under the list plays the one chosen, to hear it without opening it.

step
show: menu View
text: Now some drums. The NES plays them on the Noise channel, and the drum grid makes that easy: open View > Drum grid (it's in the Edit menu too, or press Ctrl+Shift+D).
wait: command drum-grid

step
show: drum-grid
text: The drum grid shows a bar of the Noise channel: a line for each drum, a column for each row. Click the steps to put a kick, a snare or a hi-hat on them, or press one of the beats to fill the bar at once.

  It shows the bar the cursor is in, and < and > go to the bar before or after. "Copy to every bar" repeats it all through the song. It stays open while you work; View > Drum grid hides it again.

step
show: button play
text: Play the song to hear the drums with your notes.
wait: play

step
show: button stop
text: And stop it again.
wait: stop

step
show: window
text: Two more things to try: Edit > Add chords and bass line writes a backing that fits the melody you typed, and Edit > Record from microphone turns your humming or whistling into notes.
