; Help > Tutorials. The format is in Source/TutorialScript.h.

title: Sounds and drums
about: Pick instruments from the library, and put drums in with the drum grid. About five minutes.

step
show: instruments
text: Every note plays with an instrument: a sound with its own character, how it starts, fades out and wobbles. This list holds the song's instruments.

step
show: menu Instrument
text: Open the instrument library: Instrument > Instrument library (or Ctrl+Shift+L). It lists ready-made sounds for each kind of channel in the song.
wait: command instrument-library

step
show: window
text: In the library, click a sound to hear it, and press Add to keep the one you like. Add a sound, then close the library.
wait: instrument

step
show: instruments
text: Your new instrument is in the list. Click an instrument to choose it: the notes you type from now on play with it, and double-clicking one opens it for changing its sound.

step
show: menu Edit
text: Now some drums. The NES plays them on the Noise channel, and the drum grid makes that easy: open Edit > Drum grid (or Ctrl+Shift+D).
wait: command drum-grid

step
show: window
text: The drum grid shows a bar of the Noise channel: a line for each drum, a column for each row. Click the steps to put a kick, a snare or a hi-hat on them, or press one of the beats to fill the bar at once.

  "Copy to every bar" repeats it all through the song. Close the grid when you're done.

step
show: button play
text: Play the song to hear the drums with your notes.
wait: play

step
show: button stop
text: And stop it again.
wait: stop

step
show: window
text: Two more things to try: Edit > Add chords and bass line writes a backing that fits the melody you typed, and Edit > Record from microphone turns your humming or whistling into notes.
