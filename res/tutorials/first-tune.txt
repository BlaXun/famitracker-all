; Help > Tutorials: the first one. The format is in Source/TutorialScript.h.

title: Your first tune
about: Write a few notes with the computer keyboard, play them and save the song. About five minutes.

step
show: window
text: Welcome! This tutorial shows the basics of FamiTracker: writing notes, playing them and saving the song.

  The bright frame shows where to look, and the tutorial goes on by itself once you've done what it says. Close stops it at any time.

step
show: pattern
text: This is the pattern editor, where songs are written. It reads from top to bottom: each row is a moment in time, a sixteenth note.

  Each column is a channel, one of the NES's voices: Pulse 1 and Pulse 2, Triangle, Noise for drums and DPCM for samples. The VRC6 chip adds three more.

step
show: pattern
text: To write notes, switch editing on: press the space bar. The cursor's row turns red while editing is on, and pressing space again switches it off.
wait: edit-mode

step
show: pattern
text: Now play notes on your computer keyboard. The bottom row of letters is a piano's white keys: Z is C, X is D, C is E, V is F, B is G, N is A and M is B. The row above has the black keys: S, D, G, H and J.

  Each key writes a note and moves the cursor down a row. Type four notes or more. (Delete takes one away.)
wait: notes 4

step
show: octave
text: The octave box sets how high the keys play: a higher number, higher notes. The row of letters with Q, W and E plays an octave above the bottom row.

step
show: button play
text: Press Play (or Enter) to hear your notes. The song plays from the top, and the pattern editor follows it.
wait: play

step
show: button stop
text: Press Stop (or Enter again) when you've heard enough.
wait: stop

step
show: instruments
text: These are the song's instruments, the sounds the notes play with. A note plays with the instrument chosen here when you typed it, and its number shows next to the note: red if the song has no such instrument yet.

  Instrument > Sound browser has ready-made sounds to find, listen to and add.

step
show: frames
text: This is the frame list, the song's order. Each line is a frame: a page of patterns, one per channel, played one after the other. Module > Insert frame (or the Frames number in the song settings) makes the song longer.

step
show: button save
text: Last, save your song: press Save (or Ctrl+S) and give it a name. FamiTracker songs end in .ftm.
wait: saved

step
show: window
text: That's it: you've written, played and saved a song!

  Help > Tutorials has more: sounds and drums, clips and the piano roll, and getting the music into your game.
